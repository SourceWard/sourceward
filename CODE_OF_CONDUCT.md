# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the SourceWard community a harassment-free
experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Focus criticism on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Handle security reports responsibly.
- Respect privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and irresponsible publication of vulnerabilities are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban
participants whose behavior is harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub.
